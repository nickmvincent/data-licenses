# Governance

DataLicenses.org is maintained by Nick Vincent, who has final editorial
approval for public catalog content and production releases.

Review is evidence-based and public by default. Issues and pull requests record
source questions, classification decisions, corrections, and contributor
credit. Sensitive security, privacy, or legal details may be handled through a
private channel.

Paid placement, sponsored rankings, and affiliate links are not accepted.
Contributors must disclose material conflicts of interest. Material site
funding, sponsorship, or maintainer conflicts will be disclosed on the public
About page.

Production releases require the repository's content, type, build, test, link,
accessibility, performance, and visibility checks. Cloudflare Pages publishes
the production branch after approved changes are merged. A faulty release is
rolled back with a Git revert.
