The News/Media Alliance AI Licensing Program collects AI licensing opportunities for news-media publishers. The current public page lists opt-in routes for Bria and ProRata, including use of publisher text content in AI answer products and compensation terms based on attributed use.

This entry tracks the program as a coordination layer for publisher licensing rather than as a single model, dataset, or crawler-control standard.

News/Media Alliance AI Licensing Program coordinates licensing through a collective for text and web content across the retrieval and generation stages. It also incorporates formal licensing. Coverage depends on participation, the rights represented, and the agreements reached with users. Public materials describe a currently available initiative; the newest dated source in this profile is “AI Licensing Program page lists Bria and ProRata opportunities” (July 1, 2026). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
