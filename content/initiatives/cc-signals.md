Creative Commons Signals explores ways for creators, platforms, and knowledge stewards to communicate expectations about AI and automated reuse. Creative Commons now frames the work as broader than preference signaling alone, spanning legal, technical, and governance interventions for the commons in the AI era.

Creative Commons Signals publishes or proposes a machine-readable preference signal for multimodal material across the collection, training, and retrieval stages. It also incorporates protocol standardization. The signal communicates requested conditions; compliance depends on discovery, interpretation, and voluntary support by downstream systems. Public materials describe an in-progress proposal or implementation; the newest dated source in this profile is “Creative Commons outlines signals-to-infrastructure plan” (May 13, 2026). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
