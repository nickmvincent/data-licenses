Human Native built a marketplace for licensed audio, video, and other premium datasets for AI training. Cloudflare acquired the company in January 2026 and said its team and technology would support Cloudflare's work on structured content access and new economic models for the web. Human Native no longer presents the marketplace as an independently available service.

Human Native organizes licensed access through a marketplace or exchange for multimodal material at the training stage. It also incorporates governed data sharing. Marketplace controls apply to transactions and copies served through the platform, not necessarily to copies obtained elsewhere. The initiative is archived as acquired; the newest dated source in this profile is “Cloudflare acquisition and integration announcement” (January 15, 2026). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
