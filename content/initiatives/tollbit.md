TollBit gives publishers a way to meter or monetize AI crawler access instead of choosing only between open access and blanket blocking. The company sits at the tollgate end of this catalog: part bot intelligence, part licensing infrastructure, and part marketplace for paid automated access.

TollBit conditions automated access through authentication, payment, or rate controls for text across the collection and retrieval stages. It also incorporates marketplace access. The mechanism controls a protected access route; it does not determine how acquired material is used or whether alternate routes remain available. Public materials describe a currently available initiative; the newest dated source in this profile is “Imperva integration announced” (December 16, 2025). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
