The Social License for Data Reuse work from The GovLab frames data reuse as an ongoing process of community engagement, negotiation, and accountability rather than a one-time individual consent click. Its toolkit includes structured engagement materials and sample legal clauses for documenting community-defined expectations.

For this catalog, it fills an important gap: it treats signals and agreements as outputs of governance, not substitutes for governance.

Social License for Data Reuse establishes governance for data contribution, access, and computation involving multimodal material across the collection, training, fine-tuning, and retrieval stages. Access depends on technical configuration and governance rules, including who may contribute, query, or export material. Public materials describe an in-progress proposal or implementation; the newest dated source in this profile is “Operationalization report released” (May 13, 2025). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
