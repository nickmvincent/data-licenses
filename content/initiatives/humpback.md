Humpback describes itself as an LLM paywall and analytics product for publishers. The apparent goal is to let sites see AI bot traffic, set policies for automated access, and move from unpaid scraping toward metered or permissioned use.

It belongs in this catalog because it matches the tollgate pattern directly, even if the current public evidence surface is still lighter than more established entries such as TollBit or Cloudflare. The current entry is being kept in draft while the domain history and public evidence trail are re-verified.
