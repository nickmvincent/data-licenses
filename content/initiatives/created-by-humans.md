Created by Humans is a live marketplace for books and other written works where authors, agents, and publishers can license AI rights on explicit terms. It focuses on rights-cleared access for AI developers while letting rights holders choose whether to allow training, reference, or other uses.

Created by Humans organizes licensed access through a marketplace or exchange for text across the training and retrieval stages. Marketplace controls apply to transactions and copies served through the platform, not necessarily to copies obtained elsewhere. Public materials describe a currently available initiative; the newest dated source in this profile is “About page describes mission and company story” (May 2, 2026). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
