GCX is Rightsify's marketplace for licensing music catalogs and related audio assets into AI workflows. It is a domain-specific data market platform where the rights-cleared inventory itself is part of the value proposition.

GCX (Global Copyright Exchange) organizes licensed access through a marketplace or exchange for music across the training and fine-tuning stages. Marketplace controls apply to transactions and copies served through the platform, not necessarily to copies obtained elsewhere. Public materials describe a currently available initiative; the newest dated source in this profile is “Current GCX site continues to offer rights-cleared music datasets” (July 16, 2026). These details describe the published mechanism and evidence, not a finding about legal validity, adoption, or effectiveness.
