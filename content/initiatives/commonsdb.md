CommonsDB is building public infrastructure for verifying whether digital works are in the public domain or openly licensed. It combines content-derived identifiers, signed declarations, and public APIs so that rights information can be checked across sources rather than trusted only on one platform.

That makes it an important adjacent layer for preference signals and AI data discovery: it is less about declaring new permissions than about making reuse status easier to verify at scale.
