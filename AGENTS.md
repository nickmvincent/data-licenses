# Agent Guidance

## Visible Website Copy

- Treat visible copy as product surface, not as a place to narrate implementation or design rationale.
- Keep explanations of why a UI works in final notes, issues, commits, or documentation; do not render them on the website.
- Before finishing website changes, audit new or edited user-facing strings and ask whether a site owner would actually publish them.
- Remove or rewrite meta-commentary about polish, layout, intent, or how a component is "meant to" behave.
- Avoid AI-ish filler phrases such as "underneath it," "not just," "designed to," "meant to," "on purpose," "sourceable," "reviewable," and "easy to correct" unless they are factually necessary and natural in context.
- Prefer concrete labels, factual descriptions, source status, dates, and clear calls to action.
